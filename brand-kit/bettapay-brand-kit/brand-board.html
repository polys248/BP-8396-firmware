05

Logo Usage

Always place the mark from logo/brand-mark.svg (in app code, via the BrandLogo component). The six below are the ways it most often goes wrong.

Don't stretch. Scale uniformly only.
Don't recolor. The green gradient and tile are fixed.
Don't rotate or tilt — the fold reads upright.
Don't place on busy or clashing fills. Use the approved backgrounds.
Don't add effects. Only the token shadow-glow, and only in hero contexts.
BettaPay
Don't restyle the wordmark. System stack, weight 700, no italics.
Do: use the SVG untouched on background, white, dark, brand tint or the hero gradient · keep ¼-tile clearspace · hold the 24 px floor · regenerate both brand-mark.svg and favicon.png together from the parametric builder (kept in the rebrand commit history) if the mark ever changes.
